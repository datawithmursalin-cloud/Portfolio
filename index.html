Founder · product, systems, automation

VoloLeads

A managed seller-acquisition service for US real estate wholesalers, investors and agents. I built the company and the systems under it: the website, the subscription backend, the CRM setups clients run on and the AI connectors that tie CRM, dialer and reporting together.

  • Subscription billing on Stripe with webhooks, renewal reminders and plan emails
  • Onboarding calls booked straight into Google Calendar and Meet
  • Subscriber data synced to Google Sheets for the operations team
  • CRM builds in GoHighLevel and Podio for acquisitions teams
